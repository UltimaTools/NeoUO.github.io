PathFinding

Controls the assistant's pathfinding engine: compute a path to a destination and walk or run it.

  • One-shot: PathFindTo, GetPath
  • Walk/run: Walk/Run, walkPath/runPath
  • Route-based: Go with a configured PathFinding.Route

Properties

MaxPathEntranceDistance

Syntax: PathFinding.MaxPathEntranceDistance
Returns: Int32

How far (Chebyshev) the player may be from the nearest tile of a cached road and still use it. `Go` walks the short hop onto the road from that entrance, so this bounds both the extra planning and the extra walking spent to get back onto a cached route. Beyond it the cache is ignored and a full compute runs. Raise it if your routes start from places the character often ends up a little away from (house doors, rune spots).

NearPathStartDistance

Syntax: PathFinding.NearPathStartDistance
Returns: Int32

How close to the beginning of a cached road counts as "at the start". A player is nearly always placed within a few tiles of where the route began — a rune, a house door, the spot the script walked from — so when they're within this distance of the head of the road the route is entered there and simply walked from the start. Entering at the head keeps the whole road usable, stair zones included; entering at whatever tile happens to be nearest can drop the player halfway into a route whose segments were built for a different start, which is where a long recompute comes from. Only when the player is further than this from the head does the nearest point anywhere on the road get considered, still bounded by .

ComputePath

Syntax: PathFinding.ComputePath(file, x, y, ignoreMobile, ignoreDoors)

Computes a full D* Lite road from the player's current position to (x, y) and saves it to the cache file, without walking it. Useful to precompute long routes at a convenient moment; a later `PathFinding.Go` on a route with the same `PathFile` loads the cached road instead of searching again. Returns true if a road was computed and saved.

Parameters:
  • file (String)
  • x (Int32)
  • y (Int32)
  • ignoreMobile (Boolean)
  • ignoreDoors (Boolean)
Returns: Boolean

DeleteCachedPath

Syntax: PathFinding.DeleteCachedPath(file)

Drops a cached road so the next to that destination recomputes it. A road is otherwise only replaced when a fresh plan is computed.
